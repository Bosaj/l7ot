## Open Data & Science Collaborations

We gratefully acknowledge the open data repositories and scientific organizations that power L7OT:

* **Open-Meteo Marine API** — High-resolution wave, swell, and sea surface temperature models.
* **World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS)** — Authoritative global taxonomy and AphiaID records.
* **Ocean Biodiversity Information System (OBIS)** — UNESCO-IOC marine biodiversity occurrences in Moroccan EEZ waters.
* **FishBase & SeaLifeBase (rOpenSci)** — Trophic level calculations and biological vulnerability metrics.
* **Open Food Facts** — Open product database for packaged Moroccan seafood and canned sardines.
* **Office National des Pêches (ONP)** — Official Moroccan port auctions and landing benchmark structures.
* **Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)** — Statistical fisheries catch figures for Areas 34 & 37.
